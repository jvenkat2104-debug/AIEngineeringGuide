# Help improve the guide

Students can identify confusing passages. Faculty and engineers can review their subject areas. Translators can help once a stable section and a second reader are available.

## Propose a correction

Open an [issue](https://github.com/jvenkat2104-debug/jvenkat2104-debug.github.io/issues) with the page, confusing or incorrect text, suggested change and a supporting source where relevant. A GitHub account is needed to submit an issue, but not to read this website.

For larger changes, use a pull request. Read the [contribution instructions](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and [licensing policy](../LICENSE.md). Contributors retain ownership and submit under the applicable content/code licence.

## Review rather than endorse everything

Offer to review a specific page in an area you know. Record what you checked, limitations and review date. Reviewer names and roles are credited only with consent after an actual review. No review-response time is promised.

Do not post private student records, employer secrets, identity documents or copied course material. Label personal experience clearly and keep discussion respectful.

Follow [Sources and how we check](sources-and-checking.md) and the [editorial commitments](about.md#editorial-commitments). Disclose relevant affiliations or commercial interests when suggesting resources.
