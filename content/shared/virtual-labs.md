# Practise with virtual labs

Status: Draft | Independent review pending
Official sources checked: 12 November 2026

The [Ministry of Education Virtual Labs catalogue](https://www.vlab.co.in/lab_ready_for_use.php) covers multiple engineering disciplines. Some labs require login or specific browser/software support. Individual experiments have not been tested end to end by this project.

| Path | Topics in the catalogue to investigate | Suggested learning task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Computing/AI | Algorithms, networks or machine learning | Predict outputs and compare two inputs |
| ECE | Digital logic and communication | Write expected logic or response before simulation |
| EE/EEE | Circuits, machines and controls | Identify inputs, units and expected trends |
| Mechanical | Mechanisms, vibrations and heat transfer | Predict the effect of changing one parameter |
| Civil | Surveying, soils, structures and hydraulics | Compare calculated and simulated observations |

## A practice method

1. Choose an experiment matching a subject you have studied.
2. Read its theory and procedure; check login and device requirements.
3. Write a prediction and note quantities and units.
4. Run the supported experiment, changing one input at a time.
5. Record results, an unexpected observation and your explanation.
6. Try its self-assessment and identify what a physical lab would additionally require.

Catalogue presence is not a guarantee of compatibility. Choose a supported alternative if a legacy experiment fails. Simulations complement supervised physical labs and do not validate a real engineering design. Keep your own notes with the title, URL, date, inputs, results and limitations; respect the source material’s licence.
