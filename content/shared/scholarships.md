# Find and track scholarships

Status: Draft | Independent review pending
Official sources checked: 12 November 2026

Start with the [National Scholarship Portal](https://scholarships.gov.in/) and the official scheme notice. State and institutional schemes may have separate conditions. An open portal does not mean every scheme accepts applications.

## Record each scheme separately

| Field | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- |
| Scheme | Exact name, authority, academic year and official notice |
| Eligibility | Course, institution, income, category, domicile and other conditions |
| Application | Fresh or renewal; required documents and submission receipt |
| Financial support | Award terms, renewal conditions and any authorised service charges |
| Deadlines | Separate student submission, institution verification and further verification dates |
| Follow-up | Private status record, institution contact, source URL and last checked date |

Count a scholarship in your budget only after checking its award and continuation conditions. Keep identity documents and application numbers private; never attach them to GitHub issues.

## Dated reminder: 2026–27 renewals

As checked on **12 November 2026**, NSP lists **31 October 2026** for student applications, **15 November 2026** for institute verification and **30 November 2026** for L2 verification for its named **PM-USP/CSSS and PM-USP J&K/Ladakh renewal notices**.

The student deadline has passed. The page still says applications are open; do not interpret that wording as an extension. Already-submitted applicants should check status and ask their institution about pending verification. These are not general deadlines for all scholarships. Recheck the scheme notice before acting, and treat this reminder as historical after the listed dates unless an official extension is confirmed.
