# About this guide

**Engineering Guide for Indian Students** helps prospective students and families compare engineering options, and helps enrolled students plan learning and practical work.

It has two entrances: [Choose Your Engineering Branch](choose-your-branch/index.md) and [Make the Most of Engineering](engineering-roadmap/index.md). Both are maintained in the same public repository.

## Review status

This is an **unreviewed community draft**. No independent reviewers are credited yet. Sources checked by the maintainer do not automatically make a chapter technically reviewed. Corrections and clarity feedback are welcome.

Dated deadlines, opportunities and job postings may change. Check the linked official source and exact eligibility before acting. Introductory examples do not replace a college syllabus, qualified supervision or official admissions information.

## Access and privacy

Reading this site does not require a GitHub account. There is no paid course, custom domain, subscription or AI API required to read it. The site does not add analytics, forms or advertising. Hosting providers may maintain their own access logs; external links have their own policies.
