# About this guide

**Engineering Guide for Indian Students** helps prospective students and families compare engineering options, and helps enrolled students plan learning and practical work.

It has two entrances: [Choose Your Engineering Branch](choose-your-branch/index.md) and [Make the Most of Engineering](engineering-roadmap/index.md). Both are maintained in the same public repository.

## Why this guide exists

Choosing a branch and learning through four years of engineering are connected decisions. This guide brings them together: questions for comparing real courses and costs, practical activities for exploring interests, and year-by-year learning guidance. Parents and students can use the same material to discuss options together.

Our aim is to help readers ask better questions, examine evidence and take a manageable next step. The guide complements official admissions portals, college syllabi, teachers and other learning resources. We do not claim that it is the only resource covering this journey.

## Editorial commitments

- **Free access:** read the guide and download its PDFs without registration.
- **No ads, paid college rankings or admission lead collection:** this project does not sell admission services or collect phone numbers for college referrals.
- **Evidence and corrections:** link to sources, distinguish examples from current opportunities, and explain verification limits. See [Sources and how we check](sources-and-checking.md).
- **Visible review status:** credit specific completed reviews with consent. A review of one page does not certify the whole guide.
- **Open reuse:** schools, families and contributors can adapt project-owned content under its licence, with attribution and any other applicable conditions.

These are editorial commitments, not a claim that every judgement is free of bias. Contributors should disclose relevant affiliations or commercial interests when proposing advice or resources. Recommendations should explain their educational value; payment or referral arrangements must not determine inclusion. Any proposed change to these commitments should be documented publicly before taking effect.

## Review status

This is an **unreviewed community draft**. No independent reviewers are credited yet. Sources checked by the maintainer do not automatically make a chapter technically reviewed. Corrections and clarity feedback are welcome.

Dated deadlines, opportunities and job postings may change. Check the linked official source and exact eligibility before acting. Introductory examples do not replace a college syllabus, qualified supervision or official admissions information.

## Access and privacy

Reading this site does not require a GitHub account. There is no paid course, custom domain, subscription or AI API required to read it. The site does not add analytics, forms or advertising. Hosting providers may maintain their own access logs; external links have their own policies.
