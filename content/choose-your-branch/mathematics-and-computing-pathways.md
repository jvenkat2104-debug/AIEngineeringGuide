# Mathematics and Computing: Choose the Actual Curriculum

**Level: Beginner | Draft | Independent mathematics/computing review pending**

Official programme examples checked: **13 November 2026**.

Enjoying school mathematics is a useful starting point, but degree-level mathematics may involve definitions, proof and abstraction as well as calculation. Computing adds algorithms, implementation, systems and experiments. Different programmes combine these in different proportions.

## Three routes to compare

| Route | Questions to ask about its curriculum |
| --- | --- |
| Mathematics or applied-mathematics science degree | How much proof, analysis, algebra, probability and modelling? What computational electives and projects are available? |
| Mathematics and Computing engineering degree | How are mathematics, algorithms, software and systems balanced? Which engineering/core computing subjects are compulsory? |
| Computer Science degree with mathematics options | How much mathematical depth can electives provide, and is access to those electives guaranteed? |

These are comparison lenses, not rankings. Read compulsory modules before relying on elective lists. Similar programme names can conceal different balances; the same mathematical topic can be taught at different depth.

## What study might feel like

You may work through a proof, implement a numerical method, analyse an algorithm or explain why a model fails. An illustrative week could include lectures, problem sets, programming exercises and a discussion of solutions. Verify the actual timetable and assessment balance with the department.

Mathematics-heavy study can support further study and work involving algorithms, modelling, statistics or scientific computing. Which options are realistic depends on additional skills, qualifications and eligibility. Neither a mathematics title nor “computing” guarantees software, AI or finance employment.

## Seven-day sampler

On days 1–2, explain why the sum of two even integers is even, using a definition rather than only examples. On days 3–4, implement a simple search and count its comparisons for different input sizes. On days 5–6, compare a numerical approximation with a known answer and investigate error. On day 7, write which activity you enjoyed: proving, implementing, modelling or interpreting.

No prior coding? Start with the [free SQL/Python workspace resources](../engineering-roadmap/data-engineering/workspace-and-free-tools.md) and use paper or a spreadsheet for the numerical activity. These tasks sample learning habits; they are not entrance-test preparation or an ability judgement.

## Evidence to collect before choosing

Compare first- and second-year compulsory courses, proof expectations, programming depth, project supervision and access to electives. Ask a current student for a normal problem set, not only a polished festival project. Check whether your intended postgraduate route or job notice accepts the exact degree. Account for an extra year and research expectations if considering BS–MS.

AI prompt: “Ask me to justify each step of this argument. Give a counterexample if my claim is false; do not replace my explanation with a complete proof.” Check any generated proof yourself: convincing prose can contain an invalid step.
