# For Parents: Costs, Trade-offs and Questions to Ask

Audience: parents, guardians and students choosing together
Status: Draft | Independent review pending
Updated: 12 November 2026

The aim is a decision your family can explain: subjects the student is willing to explore, a college with evidence of learning support, and costs you understand. No branch name can settle all three.

## Start with a conversation

Ask the student to explain two subjects or practical activities they enjoyed, including something difficult. Discuss the budget, travel and accommodation openly. Keep uncertainty visible: unfamiliarity with coding, English or equipment is different from evidence that a student cannot learn.

Try one branch activity together, then compare two or three actual college-and-course combinations. Family experience is useful context, but it is not a substitute for checking the student’s options.

## Calculate the full cost

| Include | Questions to ask |
| --- | --- |
| Tuition and compulsory fees | What is payable each year, and which increases or charges are disclosed? |
| Living and transport | What do hostel, food, travel and daily commuting cost? |
| Learning access | Are working computer labs, equipment and library resources available? |
| Support | Which scholarships are confirmed, and what are their renewal conditions? |
| Withdrawal | Where are the current written refund and withdrawal terms? |

Use the [cost checklist](evaluate-colleges.md) and [scholarship tracker](../shared/scholarships.md). For any loan, obtain and understand the lender’s written terms; do not assume a future salary will make every repayment affordable.

## Ask colleges for evidence

- Show us the syllabus for this exact programme and admission batch.
- Who teaches core subjects, and how can students get help?
- Can we see functioning labs and examples of ordinary students’ work?
- What support exists for language, accessibility, wellbeing and grievances?
- What are hostel/travel conditions and the process for reporting concerns?
- For the exact branch and graduating year, how many unique students sought jobs and how many were placed?
- What roles were offered, and how are salary figures defined?

A maximum package is one outcome. Ask for definitions and denominators, and speak independently to current students or recent graduates. Verify applicable institutional status and programme accreditation through official records; do not treat a promotional logo as sufficient evidence.

## Discuss the trade-offs

A preferred branch, stronger learning environment, shorter commute and lower cost may not all come in the same option. Write which trade-off you accept and why. Avoid relying on an unconfirmed future branch change or eligibility for every software job.

## A short family checklist

- [ ] The student tried an activity and read sample subjects.
- [ ] We compared the exact degree and college, not just a brand name.
- [ ] We understand total costs and any financial-support conditions.
- [ ] We checked written admission and withdrawal information.
- [ ] We recorded missing evidence and a backup option.
- [ ] We can explain the choice without promising a job or salary.
