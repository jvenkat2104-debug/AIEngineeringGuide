# Computing and Data Branches

## Computer Science and Engineering or Information Technology
In plain language: learning how computers and software solve problems and exchange information. CSE and IT programmes often overlap; compare their actual core subjects and electives.

Subjects to look for: programming, data structures, algorithms, computer systems, operating systems, databases and networks. An algorithm is a sequence of steps for solving a problem. [1]

Everyday example: an online shop needs screens, stock records, payment connections and rules for handling failed orders.

Try before choosing: write steps for a school library borrowing system. Include what happens when a book is unavailable. If you have computer access, turn a small part into a programme using a beginner tutorial.

Possible directions to investigate: software development, testing, systems, cloud operations and cybersecurity. These require different skills; they are not guaranteed outcomes of a degree.

Make college count: learn one language steadily, practise problem solving, build a small application and explain how it works. Expect debugging: finding and fixing mistakes is a normal part of programming.

## Artificial Intelligence and Data Science
In plain language: using data, mathematics and software to find patterns, make predictions or support decisions. AI, AI and ML, and Data Science titles can describe different programmes.

Subjects to look for: programming, linear algebra, probability, statistics, optimisation and machine learning. Machine learning uses examples to learn patterns rather than having every rule written manually. Official AI curricula illustrate the importance of mathematical and computing foundations. [2]

Everyday example: predicting daily canteen demand from past sales. The prediction can be wrong when conditions change.

Try before choosing: record a week of imaginary canteen sales in a spreadsheet. Predict the next day using a simple average. Compare the prediction with an invented actual value and explain the error. This illustrates prediction, not a complete AI system.

Possible directions to investigate: data analysis, data engineering, machine learning and AI application development. Some specialist or research paths require deeper study.

Make college count: learn statistics and programming, check data quality, and compare models with a simple baseline. Enjoying chatbots alone is not enough reason to choose the degree.
