# After Joining and How to Contribute

## Your first ninety days
Days 1 to 30: understand your syllabus, lab rules and assessment requirements. Meet a faculty mentor, find library and computer access, and make a realistic weekly study plan.

Days 31 to 60: try one small branch-related project or simulation. Keep notes on what you attempted, what failed and what you learned. Explain one concept to a friend each week.

Days 61 to 90: finish a small piece of work, write a clear explanation and ask for feedback. Join one useful student group. Choose your next learning goal based on experience.

Use AI tools for explanations and hints, then check important answers against textbooks or official documentation. Follow college rules and submit only work you can explain. Continue with [Your Engineering Roadmap](../engineering-roadmap/index.md) for year-by-year guidance.

## Sources and verification
The branch descriptions are introductory summaries, not a universal syllabus or a job-market forecast. The activities, decision process and checklists are editorial suggestions. Official curricula below are examples; your own college's current curriculum takes precedence.

[1] IIT Hyderabad undergraduate courses of study. Historical curriculum reference for broad subject families, not current admissions rules.

[2] IIT Hyderabad Department of Artificial Intelligence curriculum. Example of mathematical and computing foundations in an AI degree.
https://ai.iith.ac.in/btech-curriculum/index.html

[3] National Board of Accreditation FAQs. Use the official accreditation records to check a specific programme and validity.
https://www.nbaind.org/faqs

[4] National Institutional Ranking Framework FAQs and methodology. Use the current year's institutional submissions and verify branch-level claims separately.
https://www.nirfindia.org/Home/FAQs
https://www.nirfindia.org/Home/Documents

Learning starting point: SWAYAM https://swayam.gov.in/ and NPTEL https://nptel.ac.in/ . Check availability and any assessment charges directly.
