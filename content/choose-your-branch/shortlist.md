## A decision in your own words
My first preference is _____________________________________
I prefer it because ________________________________________
The main trade-off I accept is ______________________________
Before confirming I still need to verify ____________________
My backup option and reason ________________________________

Avoid a single automatic score. A serious affordability or access constraint can matter more than several small advantages. Discuss the evidence and trade-offs instead.

## A conversation for students and parents
Student: explain which subjects interested you and what you learned from trying an activity. Parent or guardian: explain the budget and practical concerns openly. Together: identify evidence still missing and agree on a next step. Friends, marketing and prestige can influence a decision; return to the student's interests and the actual options.

## If access is limited
Use a shared computer, library or school lab where available. Begin with paper activities and free learning material. Ask colleges about computer-lab access before buying equipment. Learn concepts in the language that helps you understand, while gradually practising the English technical terms used in the syllabus.
