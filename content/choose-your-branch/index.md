# Choose Your Engineering Branch

A practical guide for Indian Intermediate and Class 11 and 12 students and parents

First edition draft | 10 November 2026 | Community review pending

Choosing a branch means choosing subjects you will spend years learning and problems you may enjoy solving. Use this guide to shortlist two or three options, try a small activity, and compare the actual courses and colleges available to you.

You do not need previous coding experience, an expensive laptop, or perfect English to begin exploring. Interest can develop through practice. One exam score or unfamiliar topic does not define your potential.

## What you will find
- An interest explorer and a quick branch comparison.
- Six branch profiles with subjects, examples, activities and possible career directions.
- Guidance on broad degrees and specialised programmes.
- A college evaluation checklist and a decision worksheet.
- A small plan for your first months after joining.

## How to use this guide
1. Read the interest explorer and choose two or three branches to investigate.
2. Try their activities before deciding whether the work interests you.
3. Read the actual semester syllabus at each shortlisted college.
4. Speak to current students, faculty and a working professional.
5. Complete the comparison worksheet with your family.

This is an exploration guide, not an aptitude test, college ranking or admission guarantee. Branch names, syllabi and admission requirements vary. Check the official prospectus and counselling notices for your admission year.

Companion guide: [Your Engineering Roadmap](../engineering-roadmap/index.md), with year-wise learning and practical chapters. A separate reading website is not yet deployed.
