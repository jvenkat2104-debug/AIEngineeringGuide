# Evaluate the College and the Cost

Compare branch and college together. A branch you enjoy still needs an environment where you can learn it properly.

## Evidence to collect
- Official status: confirm the institution, awarding university, programme and campus. Check recognition, affiliation and applicable approvals through the relevant official authorities.
- Accreditation: distinguish institution-level assessment from programme accreditation. NBA accreditation is programme-specific; check the exact programme and validity period. [3]
- Teaching: ask who teaches core subjects, how doubts are handled and whether recent timetables match the published syllabus.
- Labs: ask to see student work and usable equipment, including opening hours and access outside scheduled classes.
- Support: check library and computer access, internet, language support, accessibility, counselling and grievance contacts.
- Experience: ask for examples of internships, clubs, projects and faculty mentoring accessible to ordinary students.
- Living conditions: compare travel time, hostel conditions, safety, food and reliable study time.

## Read placement claims carefully
Ask for the graduating year, exact branch, number of students graduating, number seeking jobs, number placed and whether counts mean unique students or offers. Ask about roles, location and how salary is defined.

A highest package describes one outcome. Ask for median pay and the distribution of outcomes, along with the denominator behind any placement percentage. Institute-level NIRF submissions can provide useful context, but may not answer branch-specific questions. Rankings and accreditation serve different purposes. [4]

Speak to two or three current students or recent graduates independently. Record their experience as an individual account, then look for corroborating evidence.

## Estimate the complete cost
For each option, add tuition for all years, compulsory fees, hostel or transport, food, books, computing access and travel. Record likely fee increases and one-time charges separately. Deduct a scholarship only after checking its award and renewal conditions.

For a loan, get the lender's written terms covering interest, repayment start, total repayment and charges. Compare affordability with your family. Do not base the decision on an advertised maximum salary.

Before paying, read the current admission, withdrawal and refund terms. Keep copies of official documents and receipts. Verify entrance and counselling dates, eligibility, reservation and domicile requirements from official notices for your admission year.
