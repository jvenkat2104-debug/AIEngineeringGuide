# Sources and how we check

**Status: Community draft | Independent review pending**

This page describes our editorial method and its limits. It does not certify that every linked page is current or that every chapter has had an expert review.

## Choose sources suited to the question

| Question | Preferred source | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admission eligibility, counselling and seat acceptance | The current official exam, counselling or institution notice | Year, route, programme, category, quota, round and reporting requirements |
| Programme recognition or accreditation | Relevant official regulator or accreditor, such as [AICTE](https://www.aicte-india.org/) or [NBA](https://www.nbaind.org/) | Exact institution, programme, approval/accreditation scope and validity; these are different checks |
| Institutional ranking | Published [NIRF](https://www.nirfindia.org/) methodology and results | Year, category and measures; a rank does not establish the quality of a particular branch or guarantee outcomes |
| Subjects and practical learning | The actual college syllabus, laboratory documentation, and resources such as [NPTEL](https://nptel.ac.in/) | Prerequisites, programme differences and what a student can actually access |
| Scholarships and internships | The awarding body's or employer's official notice/application page | Eligibility, location, deadline, costs and whether applications are currently open |
| Tools and technical concepts | Official documentation, established educational material and primary research where appropriate | Version, assumptions, reproducibility and limitations |

For admission portal links and route-specific caveats, use [Admissions and counselling](choose-your-branch/admissions-and-counselling.md). A portal listed here is a starting point, not evidence of an open application window or an endorsement of every claim on it.

## How a change is checked

1. Identify the claim, its intended audience and whether it can change over time.
2. Open the relevant source and check that it supports the exact statement. Prefer the current official notice to a search snippet or repost.
3. Record the source and check date for volatile information. Where important details are unclear or inaccessible, say so rather than infer eligibility, availability or a deadline.
4. Explain the scope: label illustrative activities, dated snapshots and personal experiences. Avoid unsupported promises about admissions, placement or salary.
5. Check internal links and presentation before publishing. Technical checks can catch broken local targets; they cannot establish whether advice is correct.

External websites can change after a check. An automated local-link check does not continuously verify external links, deadlines or applications. Readers should consult the current official notice before acting.

## Dates mean different things

- **Updated:** the page's text changed on that date.
- **Last checked:** the identified source or claim was checked on that date. This is not a guarantee of present availability.
- **Reviewed:** a named reviewer completed a stated review, with a date and recorded scope. Editing or automated checks alone do not qualify.

A new edit does not automatically renew an older source-check or review date. Where a page has no recorded source-check date, do not assume its time-sensitive details were recently verified.

## Independent reviews

Admissions, branch-specific guidance and technical chapters benefit from reviewers with relevant experience. Use the [review record template](../templates/review-record.md) to record the page/version, reviewer experience, sources checked, corrections and limitations. Publish names and roles only with consent after the review is completed.

All current sections remain independent-review pending unless a page explicitly records a completed review. We do not claim an established panel of engineers or educators has endorsed the site.

## Corrections and contributions

Use [Feedback and corrections](feedback.md) to report a problem with a page URL, the relevant passage and a supporting source where available. A GitHub account is needed to submit an issue; it is not needed to read or download the guide. Do not submit identity documents or private student information.

AI-assisted drafts and summaries require the same source checks and review standards as other contributions. Plausible wording, a generated citation or agreement between AI systems is not verification.
