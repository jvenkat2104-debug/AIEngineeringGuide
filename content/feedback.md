# Feedback and Page Reviews

Help us improve one page at a time. All current sections remain independent-review pending unless a page explicitly records a completed review.

## Report an error or confusing explanation

Use a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/jvenkat2104-debug/jvenkat2104-debug.github.io/issues/new?template=correction.md) with the page URL, the problem, suggested correction and source where relevant. No coding is needed, but GitHub requires an account to submit it.

If you do not use GitHub, ask a teacher, mentor or friend with an account to submit your feedback on your behalf, with your permission and without private details. A separate no-account form is not configured yet. We do not collect student documents, resumes or personal contact details through this website.

## Offer a bounded review

Offer to check an admissions page, branch pathway or student activity in an area you know. State your relevant experience, the page and what you can check. Reviewer names and roles are credited only with consent after review; one page’s review does not certify the whole site.

Use the [review record template](../templates/review-record.md). Until that process is complete, keep draft labels visible. No guaranteed response time is promised.
