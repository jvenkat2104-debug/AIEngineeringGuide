# Find and evaluate an internship

Status: Draft | Independent review pending
Official sources checked: 12 November 2026

The [AICTE National Internship Portal](https://internship.aicte-india.org/) is one official discovery route. Verify each provider’s current details: this guide has not vetted individual openings or employers.

Start with a skill you want to practise and a project showing your starting knowledge. Search by actual work, such as testing, embedded systems, power, manufacturing, construction, water systems or data analysis.

## Compare opportunities

| Check | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Provider | Employer identity and official contact |
| Eligibility | Degree, branch, year, prerequisites and location |
| Work | Tasks, deliverable, hours and mentoring |
| Terms | Duration, mode, stipend, fees and expenses |
| Timing | Current deadline, start date and availability |
| College fit | Attendance, exams, credit and placement-cell requirements |
| Portfolio | Confidentiality, ownership and permission to share results |

An official listing alone does not guarantee payment, suitability or employment. Distinguish a training course from supervised work. Obtain written terms and clarify costs before committing.

## Apply and learn

Use a concise resume and one relevant project. Explain your own contribution and dates available. Keep a private tracker with the source, checked date, deadline, response and next action. Never post identity documents or private correspondence here.

During the internship, agree on a deliverable, seek feedback and document what you learned. Publish only material you have permission to share.
