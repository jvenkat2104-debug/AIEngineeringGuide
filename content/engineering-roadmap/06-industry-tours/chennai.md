# Chennai: an industry-learning visit

Status: Draft | Independent review pending

The blueprint names IITM Research Park events and the OMR/Guindy areas. These are source suggestions, not verified current bookings or walk-in destinations. Check official event details, access rules, costs and travel needs before planning a visit.

## Before the visit

Choose a learning question: How does a product reach users? How does a team test a device? What does an entry-level engineer actually do? Ask your placement cell, faculty or alumni about an appropriate public event or introduction. Obtain permission for interviews, photographs and sharing notes.

## Three learning sessions

1. **Understand a small team.** Attend a permitted event or discussion. Ask how the team identifies user needs and shares work.
2. **Understand engineering practice.** Speak to an engineer through an arranged introduction. Ask about a normal week, review processes, testing and what a student should practise.
3. **Connect it to a project.** Draw the system you learned about and try a small related simulation or project. Document questions you still cannot answer.

## If travel is impractical

Use a public recorded talk and an arranged remote alumni conversation. Discuss what you learned with a peer, then build a small demonstration. Visiting a tech park from outside alone offers limited insight; the learning comes from the questions and follow-up work.

## Record the outcome

Keep one diagram, three observations, one skill to practise and a next step. Do not publish confidential information or assume restricted workplaces or data centres are open to visitors.
