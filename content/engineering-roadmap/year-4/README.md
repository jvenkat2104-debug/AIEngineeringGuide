## A practical study rhythm

Prepare a five-minute walkthrough: problem, diagram, constraints, choices, tests and one failure. Tailor your resume to the route you want, practise questions with a mentor and improve the work after feedback.

## Review before moving on

Can you explain the main idea without notes? Can someone follow your instructions? What did you measure or verify, and what remains uncertain? Use the [year-wise checklist](../checklists/year-by-year.md) to identify your next task.
