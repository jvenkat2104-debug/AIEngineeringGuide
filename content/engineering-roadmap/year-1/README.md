## By the end of this stage

Finish a small project, explain its parts and record what you learned from one failure.

## A practical study rhythm

Choose a topic from this week’s classes. Spend two short sessions trying an example and one session explaining it. Keep the scope small enough to finish alongside coursework.

## Review before moving on

Can you explain the main idea without notes? Can someone follow your instructions? What did you measure or verify, and what remains uncertain? Use the [year-wise checklist](../checklists/year-by-year.md) to identify your next task.
