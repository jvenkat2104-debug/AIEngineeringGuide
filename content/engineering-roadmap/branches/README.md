# Choose a branch pathway

Status: Draft | Independent review pending

Read the [branch guide](../02-branch-guide/branch-guide.md) for foundations, practice and evidence across computing, AI/data, electronics, electrical, mechanical, civil, Mathematics and Computing, and quantum-related study.

Start with your actual syllabus. Mark a topic you understand, one you need to strengthen and one you want to explore. Pick one project that connects these. Revisit your direction after completing the project rather than choosing solely from a role title.

If you are still comparing degrees, use [Choose Your Engineering Branch](../../choose-your-branch/index.md).
