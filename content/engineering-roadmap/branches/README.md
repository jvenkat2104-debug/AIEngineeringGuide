# Choose a branch pathway

Status: Draft | Independent review pending

Compare foundations, practice and project evidence below, then explore a branch pathway.

Start with your actual syllabus. Mark a topic you understand, one you need to strengthen and one you want to explore. Pick one project that connects these. Revisit your direction after completing the project rather than choosing solely from a role title.

If you are still comparing degrees, use [Choose Your Engineering Branch](../../choose-your-branch/index.md).

## Foundations to practice

Use this table as a self-check against your own syllabus, not a claim that your college omits these topics. Progress depends on practice, facilities and the route you choose.

| Path | Foundations | Practice that connects theory to work | Project evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CSE/IT | Programming, discrete mathematics, algorithms, systems | Run an application, test failures and inspect logs | An application with clear setup, tests and design notes |
| AI/ML/Data Science | Linear algebra, probability, calculus, programming | Clean data, compare a model to a baseline and explain errors | A reproducible analysis with evaluation and limitations |
| ECE | Circuits, signals, digital logic, electromagnetics | Simulate or build a sensor system and investigate communication failures | Diagram, readings and test results |
| EE/EEE | Circuits, differential equations, machines, controls | Compare simulations and supervised measurements; document assumptions | A safe power or control study with units and uncertainty |
| Mathematics and Computing | Algorithms, statistics, algebra and numerical methods | Implement a method and investigate where it fails | Correctness examples and complexity or error analysis |
| Quantum-related study | Linear algebra, quantum mechanics and programme-specific physics | Compare ideal and noisy simulated circuits | Explained results, prerequisites and limits |
| Mechanical | Mechanics, materials, thermodynamics and manufacturing | Model a mechanism and compare design choices | Drawing/model, calculations and limitations |
| Civil | Structures, soil, surveying, water and transport | Document field observations and compare simplified models | Measurements, drawings and stated assumptions |

Mechanical and Civil rows extend the original blueprint and await specialist review. Quantum-related programmes vary substantially; check actual degree content and further-study requirements.

## Choose your next exercise

1. Pick one subject you have studied.
2. Choose a small problem that uses it.
3. State the assumptions and expected result.
4. Run a calculation, simulation or safe supervised experiment.
5. Compare the result with the expectation and explain the difference.

Software and data skills can complement other branches, but do not replace their core knowledge or override employers’ degree eligibility rules.

## Routes to investigate

CSE/IT students can explore software, systems, testing and security. AI/Data Science students can explore analysis, data engineering and model applications. ECE students can explore embedded systems, communication and digital design. EE students can explore power, controls and energy. Mechanical and Civil students should develop branch-specific portfolios with faculty input. Mathematics and quantum-related pathways can include research requiring further study.

Use current role descriptions and conversations with practitioners to choose depth. No pathway here guarantees a role.


## Connect theory to simulation

Choose a [virtual-lab activity](../../shared/virtual-labs.md) matching your subject. State assumptions and predictions before running it. Compare results and limitations; simulations complement supervised physical practice.
