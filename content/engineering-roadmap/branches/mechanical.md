# Mechanical Engineering: What You Study and Do

**Level: Beginner** · For prospective and enrolled students

Status: Draft | Independent mechanical reviewer pending
Updated: 12 November 2026

This pathway introduces machines, motion, materials, heat and manufacturing. It is a starting framework, not a universal curriculum or a forecast of vacancies.

## What you actually study

Mechanics, engineering drawing, materials, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, manufacturing and machine design. Read the actual syllabus: programme names and elective depth vary.

## What practical learning looks like

Workshops, CAD drawings, measurement, materials tests, thermal/fluid experiments and mechanism simulations. Ask how often students personally use working equipment and receive feedback.

Try the [virtual-lab method](../../shared/virtual-labs.md) where equipment is limited. A simulation complements supervised physical work; it does not certify a real design.

## Try one small activity

Sketch or model a hand-operated mechanism. Trace force and motion, compare two materials, and explain a trade-off between weight, cost and ease of manufacture. Do not dismantle safety-critical equipment.

## Build through the four years

| Stage | A manageable milestone |
| --- | --- |
| Year 1 | Explain a physical system, practise foundational calculations and document a small activity |
| Year 2 | Connect a subject to a supervised experiment or simulation; compare expected and observed results |
| Year 3 | Choose a direction, complete a more substantial project and investigate relevant internships |
| Year 4 | Present a capstone with decisions, verification and limitations; match preparation to actual next-step requirements |

## Possible directions to investigate

Design/manufacturing, testing, quality, maintenance, thermal systems and related research. Entry requirements and the balance of office, factory and field work vary by role.

Use actual employer descriptions and conversations with practitioners to distinguish graduate routes from experienced positions. No salary, shortage or placement claim is made. For higher studies, check the specific programme’s prerequisites and eligibility.

## Software and AI can complement this branch

Programming, data analysis and modelling can help you investigate branch problems. Choose them because they serve a question you understand. They do not replace domain foundations or automatically make every IT job available to your degree. Validate AI-assisted calculations and designs with appropriate subject knowledge and qualified supervision.

## What to put in a portfolio

A drawing/model with dimensions and assumptions, a small calculation, test or simulation observations, a revision after feedback and a clear explanation of limits.

Separate work you performed from tutorial steps or team members’ contributions. Share only material you have permission to publish.

## Ask a student, faculty member or working engineer

- What does an ordinary week of study or work involve?
- Which foundations are used most, and what was initially difficult?
- What practical work can an ordinary student actually access here?
- Which entry requirements should I investigate for the route I want?
- What would improve this example or make its assumptions more realistic?
