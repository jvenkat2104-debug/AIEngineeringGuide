# Explore renewable-energy work

Status: Draft | Independent review pending

Energy systems involve generation, conversion, storage, networks and maintenance. Software and data contribute to monitoring and analysis; they complement electrical and physical foundations.

| Path | Example connection |
| --- | --- |
| EE | Power systems, conversion, controls and grid integration |
| ECE | Sensors, measurement and monitoring electronics |
| Mechanical | Structures, machinery and thermal systems |
| Civil | Foundations, site infrastructure and water-related works |
| CSE/Data | Monitoring dashboards, data quality and forecasting |

## Student project

Use a small provided or simulated generation dataset. Plot output by time, identify missing readings and compare a simple forecast with actual values. Explain which factors your method leaves out. Use supervised educational equipment for any physical demonstration.

## Evaluate an opportunity

Distinguish technician training from engineering internships or research programmes. Check eligibility, syllabus, practical access, charges and current availability on the official provider’s site. The source blueprint mentions MNRE programmes; their current status and suitability need verification before making a recommendation.

Do not treat a scheme name or certificate as evidence of a guaranteed engineering job.
