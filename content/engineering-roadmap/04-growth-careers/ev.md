# Explore electric-vehicle work

Status: Draft | Independent review pending

Electric vehicles combine mechanical, electrical, electronic and software systems. A possible route depends on the particular role and its qualification requirements.

| Foundation | Areas to investigate |
| --- | --- |
| EE/EEE | Motors, power conversion, charging and control |
| ECE | Sensors, embedded control, communication and battery-management electronics |
| Mechanical | Mechanisms, materials, manufacturing and thermal systems |
| CSE | Vehicle services, telemetry and connected software |
| AI/Data | Analysing battery or maintenance data with suitable domain supervision |
| Mathematics | Modelling, optimisation and control methods |

## Student project

Start with simulated voltage and temperature records. Plot them over time, mark missing data and define simple alert thresholds. Explain that this is a learning dashboard, not a validated battery-management system.

For physical measurements, use only appropriately supervised low-voltage educational equipment. Do not work on vehicle packs, chargers or high-voltage systems independently.

## Preparation checklist

Read a relevant role description, identify its prerequisites, choose one foundational topic and ask a faculty member to review a small project. Research-oriented materials and quantum applications typically require deeper specialist study. Verify course and internship availability directly before applying or paying.
