# Explore chip design and verification

Status: Draft | Independent review pending

Chip design describes behaviour and circuitry; verification checks whether that design behaves as intended. Manufacturing involves processes and equipment used to fabricate physical devices. These are related but different pathways.

ECE students can build on digital logic, circuits and signals. EE students may explore electronics and power-management topics. Computing students may contribute through software tools, scripting or relevant verification work. Eligibility depends on the role and preparation.

## Student project: a counter

1. Specify the number of states and reset behaviour.
2. Draw a state diagram or truth table.
3. Implement it in a circuit simulator or an appropriate hardware-description language.
4. Test ordinary counting, reset and boundary transitions.
5. Save waveforms and explain one mistake you found.

## Before taking a specialised course

Check prerequisite knowledge, tool access, examples of actual student work and who reviews the projects. The blueprint mentions the Chips to Start-up programme as a route through participating institutions. Check current participation and access with your department and official programme sources; no training count or access guarantee is asserted here.
