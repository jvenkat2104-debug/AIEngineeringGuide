# Year-by-year checklist

Status: Draft | Independent review pending

Use these as adaptable milestones, not a universal hiring checklist. Choose branch-appropriate equivalents with your mentor.

## Year 1: understand and finish something small

- [ ] Explain one practical system in your own words.
- [ ] Practise a foundational subject through a calculation, drawing, simulation or program.
- [ ] Finish a small project with clear instructions and a reflection on failure.
- [ ] Learn how to record changes and organise project files.
- [ ] Explain one concept weekly and join a useful peer learning group.

## Year 2: connect foundations with tools

- [ ] Build a branch-relevant project with recorded inputs, outputs and assumptions.
- [ ] Test invalid inputs or a realistic failure.
- [ ] Use relevant lab, simulation or programming tools and document versions/setup.
- [ ] For software pathways, practise Git branches, APIs and stored data.
- [ ] Write a short technical summary and get peer feedback.

## Year 3: choose depth and gain experience

- [ ] Compare two possible directions and speak to a practitioner.
- [ ] Develop two explainable pieces of project work.
- [ ] Prepare a concise resume and targeted internship applications.
- [ ] Contribute to a team or community project, including documentation.
- [ ] Arrange an industry conversation, event or remote equivalent and reflect on it.

## Year 4: demonstrate judgement

- [ ] Finish substantial work another person can reproduce or inspect.
- [ ] Explain constraints, alternatives and why you chose your design.
- [ ] Document failures, verification and unresolved limitations.
- [ ] Practise interviews or presentations relevant to your intended next step.
- [ ] Ask a qualified person for feedback and act on it.
- [ ] Compare employment, further study, research or entrepreneurship using actual requirements.
