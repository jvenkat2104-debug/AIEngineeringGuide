# Understanding AI’s impact on engineering

**Level: Intermediate** · Start with the Year 1 roadmap if these ideas are unfamiliar.

Status: Draft | Independent review pending

The blueprint discusses how AI can affect software, electronics, power and research. Treat these as questions to investigate rather than forecasts about job demand.

| Area | Application to investigate | What the student must still understand |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Software | Generating a first draft or suggesting a test | Requirements, correctness, security and maintenance |
| Data | Finding patterns or producing predictions | Data quality, evaluation, bias and uncertainty |
| Electronics | Running a model on a device | Sensor quality, power, memory and timing |
| Power | Forecasting or analysing energy usage | Units, operating limits and domain assumptions |
| Mechanical/Civil | Assisting analysis or design exploration | Physical validity, standards and qualified review |

## A learning exercise

Solve a small problem yourself, then ask an AI tool for an alternative. Compare the assumptions, steps and result. Record a mistake or unsupported claim you found, how you checked it, and which approach you can explain.

Do not put private student records, credentials or employer material into a tool without appropriate authorisation. Follow course rules for assessed work.

Keep time-sensitive claims about adoption, hiring or particular products dated and backed by current sources when adding them to this guide.
