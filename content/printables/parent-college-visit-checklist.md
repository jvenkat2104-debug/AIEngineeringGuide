# Parent College Visit Checklist

**Level: Beginner**

Status: Draft | Independent review pending | Edition: 12 November 2026

Collect evidence. Understand costs. Decide together.

## Before the visit

- Write the student's two or three branch interests and your practical budget.
- Read the exact programme syllabus and current official admission notice.

## Ask for evidence

- Who teaches core subjects, and how do students get help?
- Can ordinary students use functioning labs, computers and the library?
- What support exists for language, accessibility, wellbeing and grievances?
- Ask current students independently about learning, travel and hostel conditions.

## Understand the cost and outcomes

- Add tuition, compulsory fees, hostel/transport, food, learning access and travel.
- Check scholarship award/renewal conditions; do not count unconfirmed support.
- Read written withdrawal/refund terms and keep receipts.
- For placement claims, ask the branch/year, unique students, denominator and roles.

## After the visit

- Record the strongest evidence, the main trade-off and one unanswered question.
- Compare at least two actual college-and-course options with the student.
