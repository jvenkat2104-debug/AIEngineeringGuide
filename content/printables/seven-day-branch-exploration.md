# Seven-Day Branch Exploration

**Level: Beginner**

Status: Draft | Independent review pending | Edition: 12 November 2026

Make a shortlist through small activities, not hype.

## Day 1 - Start with questions

- Write three problems you would like to understand or improve. Pick two or three branches to investigate.

## Days 2 to 4 - Try the work

- Do one small activity per branch: paper, simulation or safe supervised work is enough.
- Record what interested you and what was difficult; lack of access is not lack of ability.

## Day 5 - Compare actual subjects

- Read the first two years of each shortlisted college syllabus. Look up unfamiliar terms.

## Day 6 - Talk to people

- Ask a current student and practitioner what they study or do in an ordinary week.

## Day 7 - Reflect and compare

- Would you spend another hour learning this? What evidence is still missing?
- Compare costs, teaching/lab access and practical constraints with your family.
