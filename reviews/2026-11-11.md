## 1. Scholarship tracking — urgent verification reminder

Source: https://scholarships.gov.in/ (checked 11 November 2026).
The homepage lists 31 October 2026 as the student application deadline for the named PM-USP/CSSS and J&K/Ladakh renewal notices, 15 November for institute verification and 30 November for L2 verification. Its wording still says applications are open despite the listed student deadline having passed. Do not advertise these as open student applications or general deadlines for all schemes. Confirm scheme-specific notices before action.

Student benefit: already-submitted applicants can ask their institution whether verification is complete; future applicants can distinguish submission and verification dates.
Targets: content/choose-your-branch/evaluate-colleges.md; new proposed content/shared/scholarships.md.
Proposed edit: “Record each scheme’s eligibility, fresh/renewal status, student submission deadline, institution verification deadline and official source separately. For an application already submitted, follow up with your institution before its applicable verification deadline. Do not assume a portal-wide ‘open’ label extends a scheme deadline.”
Priority: high, time-sensitive. No eligibility assessment or guaranteed award.

## 2. Branch-specific simulation activities

Source: https://www.vlab.co.in/lab_ready_for_use.php (checked 11 November 2026).
Official catalogue covers computing, electronics, electrical, mechanical and civil subjects. Examples include digital logic, machine learning, mechanisms, fluid mechanics, surveying and soil mechanics. Some labs require login; catalogue presence does not prove browser compatibility or successful execution. Legacy entries exist.

Student benefit: concrete practice options for students with limited lab access, especially Mechanical/Civil pathways.
Targets: content/shared/resources.md; content/engineering-roadmap/projects/README.md; content/engineering-roadmap/02-branch-guide/branch-guide.md.
Proposed edit: “Choose a Virtual Labs experiment matching a subject you have studied. Read its theory and procedure, predict the result, run it where supported, and record observations and a self-check. Check device requirements and login needs. Simulations complement supervised physical labs.”
Priority: high. Next validation: test selected experiments before marking individual activities recommended/tested.

## 3. Regional-language access instructions

Sources: https://www.nptel.ac.in/translation and https://sites.google.com/nptel.iitm.ac.in/help-center-swayamnpteltrans/nptel . Official help page checked 11 November 2026; translation landing page indexed content available, but a direct fetch returned 502.
The help page explains how to find language tabs in course transcripts and translated books when available. Coverage varies by course and language.

Student benefit: an actionable route for regional-language learners, including Telugu, instead of general encouragement alone.
Targets: content/shared/resources.md; content/choose-your-branch/shortlist.md.
Proposed edit: “On an NPTEL course page, open Transcripts and check for your preferred language. Download lecture PDFs where available, or check the Books section. Keep a small glossary pairing the English syllabus terms with explanations in your preferred language. Availability varies.”
Priority: high. Do not promise every course has translated audio or transcripts.

## 4. A practical internship discovery checklist

Source: https://internship.aicte-india.org/ (checked 11 November 2026).
The official National Internship Portal is accessible and lists student opportunities. An official portal listing alone does not establish that every opportunity is suitable, paid, currently accepting applications or quality-reviewed by this project. No specific live internship is endorsed in this run.

Student benefit: a clear way to find and evaluate opportunities rather than an instruction simply to apply.
Targets: new proposed content/engineering-roadmap/careers/finding-internships.md; content/engineering-roadmap/year-3/README.md; content/shared/resources.md.
Proposed edit: “Use the official internship portal as one discovery source. For each listing, record employer, work, eligibility, duration, mode, stipend or fees, deadline, mentor and expected deliverable. Verify details with the provider and your college. Distinguish training programmes from supervised work placements.”
Priority: medium. No particular application deadline, fee or eligibility verified.

## 5. Technology update: reproducible Python environments

Source: https://www.python.org/downloads/release/python-3150/ (checked 11 November 2026).
Official page dates Python 3.15.0 to 9 October 2026 and marks it superseded by 3.15.2. This review does not claim 3.15.2 is the newest release as of the run, and does not recommend a blanket upgrade.

Student benefit: avoids confusion when tutorials and dependencies use different interpreter versions; supports DSA and data projects.
Targets: content/engineering-roadmap/00-start-here/year-1-habits.md; content/engineering-roadmap/03-the-ai-impact/modern-developer-workflow.md; templates/module.md.
Proposed edit: “Record the interpreter and library versions used for a project. Follow your course or project’s supported version, isolate dependencies, and test a small example before changing versions. Consult official release notes and package compatibility; learning fundamentals does not require chasing every release.”
Priority: medium. Any future runnable examples need actual version testing.
